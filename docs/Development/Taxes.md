# Federal taxes
A flat 8.5 % tax rate is applied to all profit.*
# Cantonal taxes
In most cantons (all except BE,  BL, AG, VS), Corporation are taxed an additional flat rate on all profit*

As well as taxes on profit, capital is also taxed (in most cantons). This tax is proportional to the capital the company owns.

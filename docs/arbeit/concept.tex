\section{Concept}

The goal of this project is to develop a game about leading a company in a competetive, multiplayer market. The game
would have a dynamic market which has emergent properties resembling those of real economic models. Palyers would be able
to compare their companies and see which ones are the most profiable, valuable, have the most market share, etc. and
attempt to beat each other to be the most valuable company. It would be almost like Civilisation except that instead
of leading a civilisation from the dawn of mankind to hundreds of years in the future you lead a company to the next quarter.

\subsection{Core Gameplay Loop}

The game would have 2 repeating phases: the decisions phase and the simulation phase. During the decisions phase each
player would be able to make decisions that would lead their company to riches or to ruin. These decisions would include
but not be limited to:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Creating various different products targeting specific sections of the market
	\item Controlling the promotion of said products, especially which aespects of the product they promote
	\item Setting up the infrastructure to produce the products
	\item Management of personelle
\end{itemize}

During the simulation phase there would be no input by the player, instead the game would simulate the results of the
decisions made in the previous phase and compile some reports allowing the players to understand what went well or
poorly such that they can improve their decisions in the next step.

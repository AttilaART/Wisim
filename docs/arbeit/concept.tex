\section{Concept and Design}

The concept behind the game was originally to create a game where the company
creates and runs a company that manufactures and sells a product while competing
with there friends. Imagine a sort of \href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civilization_(series)}{Civilisation}
for digital entrepreneurs.

The most important part of the game is that you, as a player, have to balance
many internal and external factors: production costs, consumer demands, labour
unions, economic downturns and, most importantly, competition.

As I often do, I jumped into the coding head first, before I ever knew what I really
wanted to make. My general idea was based around \sout{copying} \textit{creating
	an experience that felt similar} to what we had experienced during Economy Week.
And since the original was just a thin skin over pretty much an Excel table,
I started off by making something similar.

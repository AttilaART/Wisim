\section{Concept}

The concept behind the game was originally to create a game where the company
creates and runs a company that manufactures and sells a product while competing
with there friends. Imagine a sort of \href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civilization_(series)}{Civilisation}
for digital entrepreneurs.

The player has to battle against forces from within and without. The player has to develop a product, attempting to
target a specific segment of the market, they have to balance its price with the cost of development and manufacturing,
all the while trying to beat out the competition.
